Data Deletion Instructions

Page Autopilot · Effective October 11, 2026

You can delete the data Page Autopilot holds about you at any time, in any of these ways.

Delete everything from Page Autopilot

  • Sign in, open Settings and choose Delete account. Your account, plans, posts, Reels and all platform connections are deleted.

Disconnect Facebook only

  • Open Connections and disconnect Facebook. Your Facebook and Page access tokens are deleted immediately and autopilot stops for all Pages.

Remove the app from Facebook

  • On Facebook go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select Page Autopilot and choose Remove. Then email us if you also want the content you created in Page Autopilot deleted.

Ask us by email

  • Email CONTACT_EMAIL from your account email address with the subject “Delete my data”. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Privacy Policy

Page Autopilot · Effective October 11, 2026

Page Autopilot helps creators plan, create and publish posts, Stories and Reels to the Facebook Pages and TikTok accounts they manage, and reply to comments on them. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you control it.

Information we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash) and time zone.
  • Facebook, when you connect it: your Facebook user ID and name, the list of Pages you choose to share, Page access tokens, your Pages' posts and their reactions, comments and shares, comments on your Pages' posts (so replies can be written), and follower counts.
  • TikTok, when you connect it: your TikTok user ID, display name, profile picture and access tokens.
  • Content you create in Page Autopilot: page plans, posts, captions, images, Reels and voice-over settings.
  • Technical logs needed to run the service, such as errors and publishing activity.

How we use it

  • To publish the posts, Stories and Reels you create or schedule, at the times you choose.
  • To reply to comments on your Pages' posts according to the reply settings you choose.
  • To show your Pages' analytics, such as followers and engagement.
  • To keep the service secure and working. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

AI processing

  • To write posts, captions and comment replies, the relevant text (for example your page topic, a draft caption or a comment) is sent to AI language-model providers. They process it only to return the requested text.
  • Voice-overs and videos are made on our own systems.

Who we share it with

  • Meta (Facebook) and TikTok, through their official APIs, to publish and read content on the accounts you connected.
  • AI language-model providers, as described above.
  • Stock photo providers receive only search words, never personal data.
  • A payment provider, only if you buy a paid plan.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

Storage and security

  • Access tokens and secret keys are stored encrypted. Connections use HTTPS. Access to data is restricted to what is needed to run the service.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account or ask us to delete your data, it is removed within 30 days. Disconnecting a platform deletes its access tokens immediately.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect Facebook or TikTok at any time on the Connections page.
  • Delete your account and all its data in Settings → Delete account.
  • Remove Page Autopilot from Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → Page Autopilot → Remove.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing us.

Children

  • Page Autopilot is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

  • If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
